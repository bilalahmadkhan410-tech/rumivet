# Clinical Safety Requirements

- The app is clinical decision support, not an autonomous diagnostician.
- Never present a database match as a confirmed diagnosis.
- Always distinguish AI suggestions, clinician assessment and laboratory-confirmed results.
- Emergency red flags should interrupt routine workflows.
- Antibiotics should not be automatically recommended solely from nonspecific signs.
- Drug dose must be linked to exact product concentration and validated source.
- Withdrawal periods must be product-, species- and jurisdiction-specific.
- Reference intervals must identify species/age/population and laboratory source.
- Suspected reportable or zoonotic diseases must display an appropriate alert and local reporting reminder.
- Every protocol should carry source, version/effective date and review date before production release.
